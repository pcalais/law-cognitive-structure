# Cognitive Structure of Large Language Models in the Brazilian OAB Bar Exam

This research project investigates the **cognitive structure** underlying the performance of **Large Language Models (LLMs)** when solving questions from the **Brazilian OAB (Ordem dos Advogados do Brasil) Bar Exam**. By analyzing LLM responses, we aim to identify reasoning patterns, limitations, and structures that resemble human legal thinking or depart from it.

## Objectives

- **Analyze** the ability of LLMs to perform on high-stakes legal reasoning tasks.
- **Diagnose** the cognitive structures and strategies used by LLMs.
- **Evaluate** generalization, consistency, and legal knowledge representation.
- **Compare** performance across different versions of the OAB exam and across legal domains.
